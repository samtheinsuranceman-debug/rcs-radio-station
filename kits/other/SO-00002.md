# SO-00002: Center of gravity, parts bin and the manager's hands

Owner: Sam. Dictated 18 Oct 2026, 03:00 Eastern. Rank: directly under SO-00001.

## 1. Center of gravity: automated workflows

The highest-order build right now is automated workflow systems that Sam programs once by talking into his recorder (the 45 web flows, with the operations manager itself as flow number one). Not new pages, not calculator fixes, not AI avatars, not making the Goldman brains smarter. Every room re-weighs its work against this. Work that does not serve a workflow is finished only if it is already nearly done, is a safety or truth fix, or clears the ground for workflows. The center of gravity can move; when it does, Sam says so to the Regional manager and the bulletin changes.

## 2. The manager sees wall to wall

The Regional Operations Control Manager owns the whole geography of the build: every project, unfinished project, started-and-abandoned project, branch, leftover package and stray file across every platform. Its job is to clean it up and get it completed in order of priority, direction and velocity. Every cycle (15 minutes, the platform minimum) it re-weighs the balance of projects, picks up the sticks and twigs (stale branches, orphan ZIPs, duplicated lanes, stale assumptions), and assigns work to rooms that are idle.

## 3. Idle rooms take assignments

A room with nothing in progress (its last restock card shows no open lane, or the bulletin closed its lane) does not wait. The bulletin's ASSIGNMENTS section names idle rooms and gives each one its next job. A room that receives an assignment confirms it on its next restock card or declines with a reason.

## 4. Deliverables rule: no download-click files, ever

No Perplexity session (and, where the platform allows, no other session) hands Sam a file he must click to download. Every deliverable is zipped, each ZIP under 5 MB, and uploaded automatically to Google Drive in the parts bin: Grok / Build / Perplexity's Part / <YYYY-MM-DD> Parts Bin. File names: PPLX_<session short id>_<nn>_<what it is>.zip, with a 00_INDEX.md next to them. The session's restock card lists the Drive link. A deliverable that is only in a chat does not count as delivered.

## 5. The parts bin is worked, not hoarded

Every cycle the manager scans the parts bin for new ZIPs and lists them in the bulletin's PARTS BIN section (new, claimed, integrated, dropped). It assigns at most one room per cycle to collect new parts from Drive and integrate them (normally North Star, which is the only seat that lands code), or it tells the room that needs a part exactly which file to fetch. A part that sits unclaimed for 48 hours goes to LAND OR DROP.

## 6. The manager's hands: pause and ask

If the manager is uncertain, sees something unforeseen, or thinks an action could do damage by accident, it stops that action, posts a HOLD comment on the Radio Station with what it saw and what it recommends, and notifies Sam. It does not guess. Notification reaches Sam's phone through Perplexity's automation push today; a text message through the app's Samuel Goldman messaging rail becomes possible only after Sam sets the messaging keys for the hosting platform and merges the Nova follow-up PR, which stay in Sam's hands under SO-03.
