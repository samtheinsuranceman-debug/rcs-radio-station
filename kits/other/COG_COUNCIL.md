# Center of Gravity Council (cross-platform triage)

Purpose: the center of gravity of the build is decided together by the platform managers, not by one of them, and Sam's word overrides all of them.

Members: the District Operations Control Manager of each platform (Perplexity, Claude, ChatGPT, Grok, Gemini, other). Chair: the Private Regional Operations Control Manager (Perplexity Computer). Secretary: the Perplexity Liaison, which carries votes in and out.

Each cycle (every 15 minutes on Perplexity; as often as each platform allows elsewhere, at least once per Sam work block):
1. Each District manager files a COG VOTE in its District Report: (a) the one lane it believes is the center of gravity right now, (b) the evidence (what is stalled, what is duplicated, what is nearly done, what Sam said most recently), (c) velocity of its own platform's lanes: moving / stalled since <date>, (d) one proposed stop-loss (a lane to stop and why).
2. The Liaison collects the votes (from GitHub where a platform can write; from Sam's paste into the Liaison chat otherwise) and posts a COUNCIL TALLY to the private Inbox: votes per lane, dissent with reasons.
3. The Chair publishes the result in the bulletin's CENTER OF GRAVITY line: consensus lane, dissenting platforms and why, and whether Sam has overridden. Sam's latest INSTRUCT ALL always wins; the council can only recommend a change to Sam with an 8-sentence briefing.
4. Any District manager can call a HOLD if it sees damage coming on another platform's lane. A HOLD goes to the Chair, which pauses that lane in STOP until Sam answers.

Format of a COG VOTE (under 120 words):
COG VOTE, <platform>, <time ET>
Lane: <name>
Evidence: <two lines>
Velocity: <moving/stalled items on this platform>
Stop-loss: <lane to stop, why>
